Baked by two friends who missed real bread

Ember & Rye started in a home kitchen in 2021. Today we bake every loaf by hand from local flour, a five-year-old starter and a lot of patience. Come early: the cardamom buns are usually gone by ten.

Why people come back

Questions

Do you have gluten-free bread?

Not yet. Our kitchen handles a lot of wheat flour, so we can’t promise anything is gluten-free.

Can I order ahead?

Yes. Call before 5 pm and we’ll hold your order for the next morning.

Do you deliver?

Bread club members in Patan get free delivery. Everyone else can pick up at the shop.
