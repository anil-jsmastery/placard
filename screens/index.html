Placard screens

The portfolio builder as it should look in the build. The app UI is placard-app.html (its CSS becomes app.css). Generated portfolios use site.css and site-render.js, the reference for the section components.

Open the prototypeSign inPortfolio sectionsPhotographer sample
  • placard-app.html every app screen, clickable. Hash routes map to app routes: #/start → /start, #/describe → /new, #/editor → /sites/[id]/edit, #/sites, #/account, #/pricing, #/sign-in, #/sign-up.
  • site.css and site-render.js the generated portfolio: themes, fonts, spacing and the markup of all 10 sections.
  • site-catalog.js section labels, icons, fields and defaults. sample-pages.js the five kinds, their example prompts and what a good build looks like.
  • site-sections.html every section with switches for theme, font, layout and width. Add ?page=designer (or developer, photographer, artist, writer) to see a sample portfolio.
