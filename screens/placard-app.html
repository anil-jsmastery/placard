      Questions people ask before their first build.

      Short answers to what people ask before they make their first portfolio.

      Who is Placard for?+

      Designers, developers, photographers, illustrators and writers who want a portfolio that looks considered without losing a weekend to it. It only makes portfolios, so every section is built for showing work.

      What does Prettify do?+

      It restyles your page: theme, heading font, layouts, spacing and photos from the library. It never changes your words. Use it on a page you built by hand, or on one the AI built when you want a different look. Undo puts everything back.

      What counts as one AI request?+

      Each first build, each change you ask for in the chat and each Prettify uses one. Editing text, moving sections or switching themes by hand uses none.

      Can I use my own photos?+

      Yes. Upload them in the editor, or pick from a library of 60 free photos. Uploads count toward your storage: 20 MB on Free, 1 GB on Pro.

      What happens to my portfolio if I cancel?+

      You move back to Free. You pick one portfolio to keep published, and the rest switch to drafts until you upgrade again.

      Billing questions

      Can I cancel Pro whenever I want?+

      Yes. Pro is billed monthly and you can cancel from your account page. You keep Pro until the end of the month you paid for.

      What happens to my portfolios if I go back to Free?+

      You pick one portfolio to keep published. The others switch to drafts and come back the moment you upgrade again. Nothing is deleted.

      What if I run out of AI requests?+

      You can still edit everything by hand. New requests arrive on the 1st of the month, or right away if you upgrade.
