Questions

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You describe your site and edit it like a document. There’s no code to see or touch.

Can I change what the AI writes?

Everything. Edit any text, swap images, reorder or delete sections, or ask the AI for changes in plain words.

What counts as an AI request?

Each time you ask the AI to build a site or make a change. Free includes 10 a month and Pro includes 300. If a request fails, it isn’t counted.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes, on Pro. Add your domain, copy one DNS record to your domain provider, and HTTPS is set up for you.

What happens if I cancel Pro?

Your sites stay live. The “Made with Placard” badge comes back and the Free limits apply again.

Where do the photos come from?

The AI picks from a built-in library of free-to-use photos. You can replace any of them with your own.
